Read: training vs sealed within a few points everywhere measurable — the models generalise; nothing was memorised. The weak spots are honest: bill_patient (patient behaviour is inherently noisy) and request_retro_auth (only 113 training examples — the root cause of the P1 failure below). write_off / bill_secondary have constant priors (no classifier); provide_requested_info was dropped below the 100-example floor and always escalates.

Do the probabilities mean anything? — calibration

When the system says 70%, it happens ~70% of the time. Expected calibration error: 0.035 on training folds (cross-fitted), 0.0495 on the sealed set — under the 0.05 bar, barely. This is the number that makes the UI's confidence displays trustworthy.

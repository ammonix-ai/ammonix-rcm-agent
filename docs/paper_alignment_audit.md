## Deviations / evidence-only items

- The paper's fine-tune training run (adapter checkpoints, 8-draft samples) is not in the
  repo; the prompts/pairs/scores JSONLs ship, and `tab:lswap`'s trained-writer row replays
  exactly from the shipped output caches. A from-scratch retrain is statistical (sampling
  at T=0.8) and needs one consumer GPU (~3 h).
- `runs/reports/three_draw_analysis.json` is a pre-rework analysis over tranches 99/101/102;
  it does not correspond to the paper's three draws (99/100/101) and is not cited by the paper.
- The architecture-level audit against the Foundation paper lives at
  `runs/reports/paper_alignment_audit.md` (2026-07-21); this file is the numbers audit.

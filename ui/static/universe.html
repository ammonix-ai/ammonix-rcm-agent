One dot

Train and test
The galaxy is the training cases, the claims the system learned from. The rimmed dots are moves on the 200 test claims it was scored on, which it had never seen. The training cases are never thrown away: they stay, as the memory every new decision consults.
Color
The kind of move. Bright means the claim ended paid, faint means it did not.
Position
Similar cases sit close together.
Labels
A neighborhood's shared move, and how often it worked there. The same move succeeds in one neighborhood and fails in another; that difference is what the system learns.

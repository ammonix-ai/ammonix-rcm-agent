The patient presents with intermittent palpitations and exertional dyspnea, raising concern for underlying arrhythmia in the context of known nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency. Given the personal history of transient ischemic attack and cerebral infarction, a 48-hour Holter monitor is ordered to evaluate for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation or other rhythm disturbances that may contribute to thromboembolic risk.

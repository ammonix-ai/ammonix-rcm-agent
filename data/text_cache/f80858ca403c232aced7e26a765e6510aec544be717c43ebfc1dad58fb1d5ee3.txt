The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness consistent with a history of supraventricular tachycardia and unspecified cardiac arrhythmia. These symptoms are accompanied by intermittent dizziness that disrupts daily activities, necessitating prolonged ambulatory monitoring to correlate clinical events with rhythm disturbances. Mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to capture real-time data during symptomatic episodes for accurate diagnosis and management.

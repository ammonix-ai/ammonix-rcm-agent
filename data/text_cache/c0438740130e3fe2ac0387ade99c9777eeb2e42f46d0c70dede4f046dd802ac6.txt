The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and intermittent lightheadedness, raising concern for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation in the setting of a personal history of transient ischemic attack and cerebral infarction. Given the need to correlate these symptomatic events with cardiac rhythm disturbances over an extended period, a long-term continuous ECG monitoring study is ordered to evaluate for arrhythmia burden and guide anticoagulation management.

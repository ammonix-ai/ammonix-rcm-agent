Following a comprehensive review of the submitted appeal, Prairie State Medicaid maintains its determination to deny coverage for the 48-hour Holter monitoring study (CPT 93226). The documentation provided fails to demonstrate medical necessity as defined by our clinical policy guidelines. Specifically, the records do not establish that less intensive diagnostic alternatives were attempted and failed, nor do they substantiate the presence of symptoms requiring prolonged ambulatory electrocardiographic monitoring. Consequently, the service is deemed not reasonable or necessary for the patient’s condition. This decision is final regarding the current claim. Should new clinical information become available that alters the medical necessity assessment, a new request for coverage may be submitted for independent evaluation.

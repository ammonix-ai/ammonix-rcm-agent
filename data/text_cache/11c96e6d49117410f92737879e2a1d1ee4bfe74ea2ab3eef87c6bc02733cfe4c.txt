The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and transient neurological deficits, raising concern for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation as the underlying etiology of a recent transient cerebral ischemic attack. Given the history of unspecified cardiac arrhythmia and the need to correlate rhythm disturbances with symptomatic events, a 48-hour Holter monitoring study is ordered to evaluate for atrial fibrillation burden and other arrhythmic activity.

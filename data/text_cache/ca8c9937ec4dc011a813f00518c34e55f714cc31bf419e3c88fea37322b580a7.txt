The patient presents with recurrent episodes of presyncope and transient neurological deficits, including brief visual disturbances and slurred speech, consistent with a history of transient ischemic attacks and prior cerebral infarction. Given the concern for paroxysmal arrhythmia as a potential etiology for these syncopal events and ischemic symptoms, a 48-hour ambulatory ECG monitoring study is ordered to evaluate for underlying cardiac rhythm disturbances.

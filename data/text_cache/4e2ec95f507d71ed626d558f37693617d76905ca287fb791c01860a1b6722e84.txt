The patient presents with a history of paroxysmal palpitations and recent transient episodes of left-sided weakness and speech difficulty, raising concern for cardioembolic events secondary to undiagnosed arrhythmia. Given the established diagnosis of atherosclerotic heart disease and the need to correlate symptoms with potential atrial fibrillation episodes, long-term continuous ECG monitoring is indicated to assess arrhythmia burden and guide anticoagulation therapy.

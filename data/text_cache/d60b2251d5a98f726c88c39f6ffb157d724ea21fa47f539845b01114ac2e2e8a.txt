This correspondence serves to notify you that the claim for CPT code 93247, representing a long-term continuous electrocardiogram monitoring study, has been denied. The denial is based on Reason Code CO-197, indicating that the required precertification or prior authorization was not obtained before the service was rendered. SilverBridge Advantage requires all such diagnostic procedures to be pre-approved to ensure medical necessity and coverage eligibility. Please note that this decision does not preclude the member from filing an appeal. The member retains the right to request a formal review of this determination within the timeframe specified in the member handbook. Supporting documentation, including clinical records justifying the medical necessity, should be submitted with any appeal request to facilitate a thorough reassessment of the claim.

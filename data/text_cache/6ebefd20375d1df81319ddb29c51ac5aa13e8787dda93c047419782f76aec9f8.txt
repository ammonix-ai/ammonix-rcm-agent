The patient presents with a history of transient cerebral ischemic attacks and prior cerebral infarction, raising concern for underlying paroxysmal atrial fibrillation as a potential embolic source. To evaluate for intermittent arrhythmias that may not be captured on standard short-term monitoring, a 30-day continuous ambulatory ECG monitor is ordered. This extended monitoring period is necessary to correlate any detected cardiac rhythm disturbances with the patient's neurological symptoms and history.

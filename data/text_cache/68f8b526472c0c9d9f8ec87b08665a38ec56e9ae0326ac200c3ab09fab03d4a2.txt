The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness, raising concern for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation in the context of a personal history of transient ischemic attacks and cerebral infarction. Given the recent transient cerebral ischemic event and the need to correlate symptoms with potential arrhythmic episodes, long-term continuous ECG monitoring is indicated to detect intermittent atrial fibrillation and guide anticoagulation therapy.

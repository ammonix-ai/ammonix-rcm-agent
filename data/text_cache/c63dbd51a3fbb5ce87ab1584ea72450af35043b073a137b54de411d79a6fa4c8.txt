The patient presents with a history of sick sinus syndrome and atrial fibrillation, complicated by recent episodes of transient cerebral ischemic attacks. They report recurrent palpitations, dizziness, and intermittent lightheadedness that are poorly correlated with standard Holter monitoring intervals. Mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to capture arrhythmic events in real-time and correlate them with symptomatic episodes to guide rate and rhythm control management.

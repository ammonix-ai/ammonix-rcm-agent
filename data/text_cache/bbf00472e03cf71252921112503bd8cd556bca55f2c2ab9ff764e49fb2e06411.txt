The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and transient neurological deficits, consistent with a history of paroxysmal atrial fibrillation and prior transient ischemic attacks. Given the need to correlate these symptomatic events with underlying arrhythmia, a cardiac event monitor is ordered to capture intermittent atrial fibrillation episodes. This monitoring is essential to guide anticoagulation therapy and assess the burden of atrial fibrillation in the context of the patient's cerebrovascular history.

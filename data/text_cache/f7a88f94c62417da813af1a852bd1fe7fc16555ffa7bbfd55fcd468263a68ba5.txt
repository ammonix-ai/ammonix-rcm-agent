The patient presents with recurrent episodes of transient neurological deficits and palpitations, prompting an evaluation for underlying paroxysmal arrhythmias in the context of a personal history of transient ischemic attack and cerebral infarction. Given the current diagnosis of unspecified cardiac arrhythmia and the need to correlate potential atrial fibrillation with prior cerebrovascular events, a long-term continuous ECG monitoring study is ordered to capture intermittent rhythm disturbances.

The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness, raising concern for an underlying cardiac arrhythmia in the context of a personal history of transient ischemic attacks and cerebral infarction. Given the intermittent nature of these symptoms and the need to correlate potential rhythm disturbances with recent transient cerebral ischemic events, mobile cardiac telemetry monitoring is ordered to capture continuous electrocardiographic data over an extended period.

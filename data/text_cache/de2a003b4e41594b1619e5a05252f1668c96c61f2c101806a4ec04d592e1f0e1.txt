The patient presents with recurrent episodes of presyncope and transient loss of consciousness, accompanied by palpitations suggestive of paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia. Given the personal history of transient ischemic attack and cerebral infarction, continuous ambulatory ECG monitoring is indicated to evaluate for occult arrhythmias that may be contributing to the syncopal events and to assess for potential cardioembolic sources.

The patient presents with a history of sick sinus syndrome and atrial fibrillation, reporting recurrent episodes of palpitations, dizziness, and transient neurological deficits suggestive of prior transient cerebral ischemic attacks. A 48-hour Holter monitor is ordered to evaluate for arrhythmia burden, assess the efficacy of current rate control therapy, and investigate potential cardioembolic sources for the reported transient ischemic symptoms.

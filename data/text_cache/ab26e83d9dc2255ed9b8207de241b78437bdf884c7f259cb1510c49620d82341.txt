The patient presents with recurrent palpitations and a history of atrial fibrillation, raising concern for intermittent arrhythmia recurrence. Given the personal history of transient ischemic attack and cerebral infarction, continuous monitoring is indicated to detect asymptomatic or symptomatic atrial fibrillation episodes that may increase stroke risk. Mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to capture rhythm data over an extended period to guide anticoagulation and rate control management.

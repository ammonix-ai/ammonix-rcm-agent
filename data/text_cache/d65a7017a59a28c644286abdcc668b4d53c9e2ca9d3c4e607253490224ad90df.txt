The patient presents with recurrent episodes of dizziness and giddiness, raising concern for paroxysmal arrhythmias in the context of a personal history of transient ischemic attack and cerebral infarction. Given the concurrent diagnosis of nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency, long-term continuous ECG monitoring is indicated to evaluate for atrial fibrillation or other rhythm disturbances that may contribute to these symptoms and increase stroke risk.

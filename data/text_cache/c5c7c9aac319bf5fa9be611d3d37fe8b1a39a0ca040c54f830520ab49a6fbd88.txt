The patient presents with a history of paroxysmal atrial fibrillation and recurrent episodes of palpitations, accompanied by recent transient neurological symptoms suggestive of transient cerebral ischemic attacks. Due to the need to correlate these intermittent arrhythmic events with potential embolic phenomena and optimize rate control, a 30-day continuous ambulatory ECG monitoring study is ordered to capture the frequency and duration of atrial fibrillation episodes.

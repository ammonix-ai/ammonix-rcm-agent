Following a comprehensive review of the submitted appeal materials, Harborview Medicaid maintains its determination to deny coverage for the long-term continuous electrocardiogram monitoring study, CPT code 93247. Our clinical assessment indicates that the requested service does not meet the established medical necessity criteria for this specific diagnosis. Consequently, the prior denial stands. We encourage the provider to submit additional peer-reviewed literature or specific clinical evidence that directly supports the necessity of this extended monitoring protocol for future consideration.

The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations, lightheadedness, and near-syncope that are clinically consistent with a history of sick sinus syndrome and paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia. Given the intermittent nature of these symptoms and the need to correlate specific arrhythmic events with clinical complaints, a long-term continuous ECG monitoring study is indicated to characterize the underlying cardiac arrhythmia.

The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness, raising clinical suspicion for paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia. Given the intermittent nature of the symptoms and the need to correlate specific arrhythmic events with the patient's subjective complaints, a mobile cardiac telemetry study is ordered to capture diagnostic data. This monitoring will help characterize the underlying cardiac arrhythmia and guide subsequent management strategies.

Following a comprehensive review of the submitted appeal, Harborview Medicaid maintains its determination to deny coverage for the 48-hour Holter monitoring study (CPT 93226). The clinical documentation provided fails to demonstrate medical necessity as defined by current coverage policies. Specifically, the records do not substantiate that less intensive diagnostic alternatives were attempted and failed, nor do they establish a clear correlation between the patient’s symptoms and the need for prolonged ambulatory electrocardiographic monitoring. Consequently, the service is deemed experimental or investigational under the applicable benefit plan. This decision is final regarding the current claim submission. Should new clinical evidence emerge that directly addresses these specific criteria, a new request for coverage may be submitted for independent evaluation.

The patient presents with intermittent palpitations and a recent history of transient cerebral ischemic attacks, raising concern for an underlying arrhythmic etiology such as paroxysmal atrial fibrillation. Given the episodic nature of the symptoms and the need to correlate cardiac rhythm with neurological events, a 30-day continuous ECG monitoring study is ordered to capture potential arrhythmias that may have been missed on standard monitoring.

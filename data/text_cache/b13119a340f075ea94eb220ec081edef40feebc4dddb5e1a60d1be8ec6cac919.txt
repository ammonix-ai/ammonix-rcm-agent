The patient presents with a history of paroxysmal palpitations and recent transient episodes of visual disturbance and speech difficulty suggestive of transient cerebral ischemic attacks. Given the known diagnosis of unspecified atrial fibrillation and underlying atherosclerotic heart disease of native coronary arteries, a 48-hour Holter monitor is ordered to assess for arrhythmia burden and correlate symptoms with cardiac rhythm disturbances.

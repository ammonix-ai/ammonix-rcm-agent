The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness, accompanied by a history of documented supraventricular tachycardia and paroxysmal atrial fibrillation. Recent syncopal events have raised concern for intermittent high-grade arrhythmias that are not captured by standard Holter monitoring. Mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to provide continuous rhythm correlation with symptoms over an extended period to guide further management.

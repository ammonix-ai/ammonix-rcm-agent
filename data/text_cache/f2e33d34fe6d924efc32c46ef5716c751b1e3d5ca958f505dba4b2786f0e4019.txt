This notice serves to inform you that Prairie State Medicaid has denied the claim for mobile cardiac telemetry monitoring (CPT 93229) due to reason code CO-16: Claim lacks information or has submission error. Please submit your appeal request within the timeframe specified in your member handbook, ensuring all supporting evidence is attached to facilitate a timely review of your case.

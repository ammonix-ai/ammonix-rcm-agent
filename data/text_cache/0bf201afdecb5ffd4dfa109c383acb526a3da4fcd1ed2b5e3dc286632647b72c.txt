The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness, raising concern for supraventricular tachycardia in the context of a personal history of transient ischemic attacks and cerebral infarction. Given the potential hemodynamic impact of arrhythmias on cerebral perfusion and the need to correlate symptoms with rhythm disturbances, mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to capture intermittent events over an extended period. This monitoring will assist in determining the frequency and duration of tachyarrhythmias to guide appropriate anticoagulation and antiarrhythmic management.

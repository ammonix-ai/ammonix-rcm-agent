The patient presents with a history of transient cerebral ischemic attacks and atherosclerotic heart disease, reporting recent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness that raise concern for an underlying arrhythmia. Given the personal history of TIA and cerebral infarction, a 48-hour Holter monitor is ordered to evaluate for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation or other rhythm disturbances that may contribute to embolic risk.

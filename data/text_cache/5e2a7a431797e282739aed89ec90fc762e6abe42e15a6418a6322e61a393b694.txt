The patient presents with recurrent episodes of transient neurological deficits consistent with a recent transient ischemic attack, occurring in the context of a known personal history of TIA and cerebral infarction. Given the concurrent diagnosis of nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency, there is clinical concern for potential paroxysmal atrial fibrillation or other arrhythmias contributing to embolic risk. A mobile cardiac telemetry study is ordered to evaluate for occult arrhythmias that may be precipitating these ischemic events.

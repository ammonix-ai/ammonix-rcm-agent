The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness in the setting of known paroxysmal atrial fibrillation and a significant history of transient ischemic attacks. Given the concern for intermittent arrhythmia contributing to prior cerebral ischemic events, long-term continuous ECG monitoring is ordered to detect asymptomatic or symptomatic atrial fibrillation burden and guide anticoagulation management.

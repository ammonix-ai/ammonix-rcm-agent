The patient presents with recurrent episodes of unexplained syncope and transient neurological deficits suggestive of transient cerebral ischemic attacks, occurring in the context of known atherosclerotic heart disease of the native coronary arteries. Given the intermittent and unpredictable nature of these symptoms, long-term continuous ECG monitoring is indicated to detect potential arrhythmic causes or ischemic changes that may not be captured on standard ambulatory monitoring.

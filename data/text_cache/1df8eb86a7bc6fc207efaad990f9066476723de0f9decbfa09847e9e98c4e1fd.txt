This correspondence serves to notify you that the claim for Mobile Cardiac Telemetry monitoring (CPT 93229) has been denied. The denial is based on Reason Code CO-197, indicating that the required precertification or prior authorization was not obtained before services were rendered. SilverBridge Advantage requires all such diagnostic monitoring services to be pre-approved to ensure medical necessity and coverage eligibility. Please note that this decision does not preclude the provider from submitting a corrected claim if valid authorization is subsequently secured. The member retains the right to appeal this determination. Detailed instructions regarding the formal appeal process, including necessary documentation and submission deadlines, are available in the attached Explanation of Benefits or via our member services portal.

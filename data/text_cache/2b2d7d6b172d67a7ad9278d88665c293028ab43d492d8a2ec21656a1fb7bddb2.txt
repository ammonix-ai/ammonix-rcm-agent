The patient presents with a history of sick sinus syndrome, supraventricular tachycardia, and unspecified atrial fibrillation, reporting recurrent episodes of palpitations, dizziness, and intermittent lightheadedness. Due to the paroxysmal nature of these symptoms and the need to correlate clinical events with rhythm disturbances, a cardiac event monitoring study is ordered to evaluate for arrhythmia burden and guide further management.

This correspondence serves to notify you that the claim for CPT code 93247, representing a long-term continuous electrocardiographic monitoring study, has been denied. The denial is based on Reason Code CO-197, indicating that the required precertification or prior authorization was not obtained or submitted prior to the service date. Coverage for this procedure mandates advance approval to ensure medical necessity and compliance with plan guidelines. Please be advised that the member retains the right to appeal this determination. Detailed instructions regarding the appeals process, including necessary documentation and submission deadlines, are available in the member’s Summary of Benefits and Coverage or by contacting Member Services directly. We encourage you to review these rights promptly to facilitate any potential reconsideration of this claim.

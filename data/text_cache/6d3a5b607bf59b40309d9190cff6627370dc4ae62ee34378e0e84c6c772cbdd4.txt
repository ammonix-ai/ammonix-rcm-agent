Following a comprehensive review of the submitted appeal, Prairie State Medicaid maintains its determination to deny coverage for the 48-hour Holter monitoring study (CPT 93226). The provided clinical documentation fails to demonstrate medical necessity as defined by current state Medicaid guidelines. Specifically, the records do not substantiate that less intensive diagnostic alternatives were attempted and failed, nor do they establish a clear correlation between the patient’s symptoms and the need for prolonged ambulatory electrocardiographic monitoring. Consequently, the service is deemed not reasonable or necessary for the diagnosis or treatment of the patient’s condition. This decision is final regarding the current claim submission.

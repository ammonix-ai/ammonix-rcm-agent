The patient presents with a history of transient ischemic attacks and cerebral infarction, prompting evaluation for potential paroxysmal atrial fibrillation as a contributing etiology. Given the concurrent diagnosis of nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency, which increases the risk for left atrial enlargement and arrhythmia, continuous ambulatory monitoring is indicated to detect occult atrial fibrillation episodes that may not be captured on standard 12-lead ECG.

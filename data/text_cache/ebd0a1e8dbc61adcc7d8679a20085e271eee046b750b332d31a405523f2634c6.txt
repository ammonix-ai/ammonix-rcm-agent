The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and intermittent lightheadedness, raising concern for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation in the setting of known atherosclerotic heart disease. Given the need to correlate symptoms with rhythm disturbances and assess the efficacy of current rate control therapy, mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to capture continuous electrocardiographic data during daily activities. This monitoring will help distinguish between symptomatic arrhythmias and benign ectopy while evaluating for potential ischemic changes associated with underlying coronary artery disease.

The patient presents with a recent episode of transient neurological deficits consistent with a transient cerebral ischemic attack, occurring in the context of a known personal history of TIA and cerebral infarction. Given the concurrent diagnosis of nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency, there is concern for potential cardioembolic sources contributing to the ischemic events. Mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to evaluate for paroxysmal arrhythmias that may be precipitating these episodes.

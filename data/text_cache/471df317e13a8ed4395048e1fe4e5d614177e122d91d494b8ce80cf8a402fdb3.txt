The patient presents with a history of sick sinus syndrome and recurrent episodes of supraventricular tachycardia, complicated by unspecified atrial fibrillation. They report persistent palpitations, intermittent dizziness, and unexplained syncope that have not been adequately characterized by standard resting electrocardiograms. A cardiac event monitor is ordered to capture the arrhythmia burden and correlate symptoms with rhythm disturbances for further management.

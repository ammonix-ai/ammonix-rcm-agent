Following a comprehensive review of the submitted appeal materials, Prairie State Medicaid maintains its determination to deny coverage for the long-term continuous electrocardiogram monitoring study, CPT code 93247. The clinical documentation provided fails to demonstrate the requisite medical necessity as defined by current state Medicaid coverage criteria. Specifically, the records do not substantiate that less intensive monitoring options were attempted and failed, nor do they establish a clear diagnostic uncertainty that necessitates this specific level of prolonged surveillance. Consequently, the prior denial stands. Please refer to the attached coverage policy guidelines for detailed requirements regarding cardiac monitoring services. Should additional clinical evidence become available that addresses these specific deficiencies, a new claim may be submitted for reconsideration.

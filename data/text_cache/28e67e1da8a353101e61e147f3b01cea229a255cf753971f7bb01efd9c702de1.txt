The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and intermittent lightheadedness consistent with paroxysmal atrial fibrillation and underlying sick sinus syndrome. Due to the unpredictable nature of these arrhythmic events and the need to correlate symptoms with rhythm disturbances over an extended period, a long-term continuous ECG monitoring study is indicated to optimize rate control and assess the efficacy of current antiarrhythmic therapy.

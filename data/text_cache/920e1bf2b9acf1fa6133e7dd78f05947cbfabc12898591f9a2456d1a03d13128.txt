The patient presents with a history of sick sinus syndrome and recurrent supraventricular tachycardia, complicated by episodes of unspecified atrial fibrillation. They report persistent palpitations, intermittent dizziness, and unexplained syncope that have not been adequately characterized by standard short-term monitoring. A long-term continuous ECG monitoring study is ordered to correlate these symptomatic events with underlying arrhythmic activity and guide further management.

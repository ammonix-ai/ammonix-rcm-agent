The patient presents with a history of atherosclerotic heart disease and recent transient cerebral ischemic attacks, accompanied by intermittent palpitations and presyncope suggestive of an underlying arrhythmia. Given the need to correlate these episodic symptoms with potential cardiac rhythm disturbances over an extended period, a long-term continuous ECG monitoring study is indicated to evaluate for occult atrial fibrillation or other significant arrhythmias.

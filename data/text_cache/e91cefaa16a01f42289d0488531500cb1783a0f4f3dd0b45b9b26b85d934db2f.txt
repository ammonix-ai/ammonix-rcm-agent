Following a comprehensive review of the submitted appeal materials, Cornerstone United maintains its determination to deny coverage for the cardiac event monitoring study (CPT 93271). Our clinical assessment indicates that the requested service does not meet the established medical necessity criteria outlined in our coverage policy. Specifically, the documentation provided fails to demonstrate that less intensive monitoring options were attempted and failed prior to initiating this level of diagnostic testing. Consequently, the claim remains denied as experimental or investigational under the current plan provisions. This decision is final and constitutes the end of the administrative appeal process for this specific claim.

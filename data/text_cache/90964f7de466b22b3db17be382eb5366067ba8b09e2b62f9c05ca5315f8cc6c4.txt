The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness consistent with sick sinus syndrome, occurring in the context of known atherosclerotic heart disease. Recent transient cerebral ischemic attacks raise concern for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation or other arrhythmias that may be contributing to embolic events. Mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to capture intermittent rhythm disturbances over an extended period to guide appropriate anticoagulation and rate control management.

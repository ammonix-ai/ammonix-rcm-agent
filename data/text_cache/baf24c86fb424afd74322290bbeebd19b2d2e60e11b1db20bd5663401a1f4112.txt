The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and transient neurological deficits, consistent with a history of paroxysmal atrial fibrillation and prior transient ischemic attacks. Given the need to correlate these symptomatic events with underlying cardiac rhythm disturbances, a cardiac event monitor is ordered to capture intermittent arrhythmias. This study will aid in evaluating the burden of atrial fibrillation and assessing the potential embolic risk associated with the patient's history of cerebral infarction.

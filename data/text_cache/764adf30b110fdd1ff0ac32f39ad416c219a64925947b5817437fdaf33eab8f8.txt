The patient presents with recurrent episodes of transient neurological deficits consistent with transient ischemic attacks, occurring in the context of established atherosclerotic heart disease and a personal history of prior TIA and cerebral infarction. Given the high suspicion for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation or other arrhythmias as a potential embolic source, long-term continuous ECG monitoring is ordered to detect intermittent cardiac rhythm disturbances that may not be captured on standard ambulatory monitoring.

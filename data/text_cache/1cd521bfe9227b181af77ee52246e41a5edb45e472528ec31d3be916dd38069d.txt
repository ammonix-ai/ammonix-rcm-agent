The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations, dizziness, and near-syncope that are poorly correlated with standard short-term monitoring, raising concern for intermittent sick sinus syndrome and supraventricular tachycardia. Given the history of unexplained cardiac arrhythmias and the need to capture infrequent symptomatic events over an extended period, a long-term continuous ECG monitoring study is indicated to establish a definitive diagnosis and guide management.

The patient presents with recurrent episodes of dizziness and transient visual disturbances concerning for transient cerebral ischemic attacks, alongside a known history of sick sinus syndrome and unspecified cardiac arrhythmia. To evaluate for potential paroxysmal atrial fibrillation or high-grade atrioventricular block that may be contributing to these cerebral hypoperfusion events, a 48-hour ambulatory electrocardiographic monitoring study is indicated.

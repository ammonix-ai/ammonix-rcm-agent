This correspondence serves to notify you that the claim for Mobile Cardiac Telemetry monitoring (CPT 93229) has been denied. The denial is based on Reason Code CO-197, indicating that the required precertification or prior authorization was not obtained before services were rendered. SilverBridge Advantage requires all non-emergency diagnostic monitoring services to be pre-approved to ensure medical necessity and coverage eligibility. As the provider, you have the right to appeal this determination. Please submit a formal appeal within the timeframe specified in your provider agreement, including any supporting clinical documentation that justifies the medical necessity of the service. Failure to adhere to these requirements may result in the finalization of this denial.

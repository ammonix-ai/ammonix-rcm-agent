The patient presents with a history of transient cerebral ischemic attacks accompanied by palpitations and episodic lightheadedness, raising concern for an underlying cardiac arrhythmia as the potential embolic source. To correlate these neurological symptoms with cardiac rhythm disturbances, mobile cardiac telemetry monitoring is ordered to capture intermittent events over an extended period. This continuous monitoring will assist in identifying any paroxysmal arrhythmias that may have been missed during standard office evaluations.

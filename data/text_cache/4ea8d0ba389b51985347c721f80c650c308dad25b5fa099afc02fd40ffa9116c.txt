Following a comprehensive review of the submitted appeal materials, SilverBridge Advantage maintains its determination to deny coverage for the mobile cardiac telemetry monitoring study (CPT 93229). The clinical documentation provided fails to demonstrate the requisite medical necessity criteria as defined by our coverage policies. Specifically, the records do not substantiate that less intensive monitoring options were attempted and deemed insufficient prior to initiating this level of care. Consequently, the request does not meet the established standards for reimbursement. This decision is final regarding the current claim submission. Should additional clinical evidence become available that directly addresses the cited deficiencies, a new claim may be submitted for reconsideration.

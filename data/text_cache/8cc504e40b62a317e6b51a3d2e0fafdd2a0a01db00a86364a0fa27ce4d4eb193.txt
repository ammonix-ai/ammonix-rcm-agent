The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness, raising concern for paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia and other unspecified cardiac arrhythmias. Given the intermittent nature of these symptoms and the associated dizziness, mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to capture real-time rhythm data during daily activities. This extended monitoring will help correlate the patient's subjective complaints with objective electrocardiographic findings to guide further management.

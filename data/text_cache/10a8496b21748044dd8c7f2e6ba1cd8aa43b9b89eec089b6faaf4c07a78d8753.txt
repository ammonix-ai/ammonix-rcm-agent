Following a comprehensive review of the submitted clinical documentation and applicable coverage guidelines, we affirm the initial determination regarding the 48-hour Holter monitoring study (CPT 93226). The provided records do not sufficiently demonstrate medical necessity as defined by current federal Medicare policies. Specifically, the clinical indication lacks the requisite evidence of symptomatic arrhythmia or diagnostic uncertainty that would justify extended ambulatory electrocardiographic monitoring. Consequently, the appeal is denied, and the claim remains non-covered. Please note that this decision is final under the current administrative review process. Should new, substantiating clinical information become available, a new claim may be submitted for reconsideration.

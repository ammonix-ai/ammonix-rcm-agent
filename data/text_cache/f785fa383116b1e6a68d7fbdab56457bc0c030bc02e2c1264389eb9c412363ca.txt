The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and exertional dyspnea, consistent with a history of paroxysmal atrial fibrillation and underlying nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency. Given the recent increase in arrhythmic symptoms and the need to characterize the burden of atrial ectopy, a cardiac event monitoring study is ordered to evaluate for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation and other unspecified cardiac arrhythmias.

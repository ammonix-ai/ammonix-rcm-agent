The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and exertional dyspnea, raising clinical suspicion for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation in the context of a prior diagnosis of unspecified atrial fibrillation. A 48-hour ambulatory ECG monitoring study is ordered to characterize the arrhythmia burden and correlate rhythm changes with symptomatic events. This evaluation is necessary to guide further management of the underlying cardiac arrhythmia.

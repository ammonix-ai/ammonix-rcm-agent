The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and exertional dyspnea, prompting evaluation for underlying arrhythmia and valvular pathology. History is significant for nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency and a prior diagnosis of atrial fibrillation, which has been clinically unstable. Mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to characterize the rhythm and correlate symptoms with potential paroxysmal atrial fibrillation episodes.

The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness, consistent with a history of supraventricular tachycardia, alongside a recent transient cerebral ischemic attack and prior history of TIA and cerebral infarction. Mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to correlate these paroxysmal symptoms with underlying arrhythmia and assess for potential cardioembolic sources contributing to the patient's cerebrovascular risk profile.

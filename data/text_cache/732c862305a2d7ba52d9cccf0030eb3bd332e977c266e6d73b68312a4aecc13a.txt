The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and intermittent dizziness, raising concern for paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia and other unspecified cardiac arrhythmias. Due to the sporadic nature of these symptoms, standard short-term monitoring has been insufficient to capture the underlying rhythm disturbances. A long-term continuous ECG monitoring study is ordered to correlate the patient's reported symptoms with specific cardiac events for accurate diagnosis and management.

The patient presents with a history of paroxysmal atrial fibrillation and supraventricular tachycardia, complicated by a recent transient cerebral ischemic attack. They report recurrent episodes of palpitations, lightheadedness, and intermittent chest discomfort that are difficult to capture on standard ambulatory monitoring. A cardiac event monitor is ordered to correlate these symptomatic episodes with underlying arrhythmias and guide further management.

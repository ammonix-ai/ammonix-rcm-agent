The patient presents with recurrent episodes of syncope and collapse in the setting of known paroxysmal atrial fibrillation and a personal history of transient ischemic attack and cerebral infarction. Due to the high risk of thromboembolic events and the need to correlate intermittent symptoms with potential arrhythmic activity, long-term continuous ECG monitoring is indicated to evaluate for occult atrial fibrillation burden and guide anticoagulation therapy.

The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and intermittent lightheadedness, raising concern for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation in the setting of known atherosclerotic heart disease. Mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to capture asymptomatic and symptomatic arrhythmic events over an extended period to guide rate and rhythm control management. This monitoring will help correlate symptoms with underlying atrial fibrillation episodes and assess the efficacy of current therapeutic interventions.

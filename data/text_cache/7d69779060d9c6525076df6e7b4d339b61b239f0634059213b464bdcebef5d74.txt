The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness, raising concern for paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia in the context of a personal history of transient ischemic attacks and cerebral infarction. Given the intermittent nature of these symptoms and the need to evaluate for potential arrhythmic contributors to prior cerebrovascular events, long-term continuous ECG monitoring is indicated to capture any underlying rhythm disturbances.

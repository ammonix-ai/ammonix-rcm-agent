The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and a sensation of rapid heart rate, raising concern for paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia. Given the intermittent nature of these symptoms and the need to correlate clinical events with rhythm disturbances, mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to capture continuous electrocardiographic data over an extended period. This monitoring will assist in confirming the diagnosis of unspecified cardiac arrhythmia and guiding appropriate therapeutic management.

The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness, raising concern for paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia in the context of a personal history of transient ischemic attacks and cerebral infarction. Given the intermittent nature of these symptoms and the need to evaluate for potential arrhythmic causes of cerebral hypoperfusion, a cardiac event monitoring study is ordered to capture rhythm data during symptomatic episodes.

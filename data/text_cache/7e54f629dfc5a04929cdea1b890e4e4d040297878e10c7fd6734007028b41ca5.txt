The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and intermittent shortness of breath, raising concern for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation in the setting of known nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency. To further characterize the arrhythmia burden and correlate symptoms with rhythm disturbances, a cardiac event monitoring study is ordered. This evaluation will assist in determining the appropriate management strategy for the patient's unspecified atrial fibrillation and associated valvular disease.

The patient presents with a recent episode of transient neurological deficits consistent with a transient ischemic attack, alongside a personal history of prior TIA and cerebral infarction. Given the current complaint of palpitations and the need to evaluate for underlying cardiac arrhythmia as a potential embolic source, a 48-hour Holter monitor is ordered to assess for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation or other rhythm disturbances.

The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and transient neurological symptoms, including brief visual disturbances and mild aphasia, raising concern for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation contributing to transient cerebral ischemic attacks. Given the personal history of TIA and cerebral infarction, mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to correlate these intermittent symptoms with underlying arrhythmias and guide appropriate anticoagulation and rate control management.

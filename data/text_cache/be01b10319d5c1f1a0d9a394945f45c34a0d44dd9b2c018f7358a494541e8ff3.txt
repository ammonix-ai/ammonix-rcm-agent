The patient presents with a history of paroxysmal atrial fibrillation and recent episodes of transient cerebral ischemic attacks, raising concern for an underlying embolic source. To evaluate for occult arrhythmias and correlate symptoms with cardiac rhythm disturbances, long-term continuous ECG monitoring is indicated. This study will assist in determining the efficacy of current anticoagulation therapy and guiding further management of the unspecified cardiac arrhythmia.

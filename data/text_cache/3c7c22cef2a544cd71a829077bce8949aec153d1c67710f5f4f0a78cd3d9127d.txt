The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness, raising concern for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation and supraventricular tachycardia. Given the intermittent nature of these symptoms and the need to correlate clinical events with rhythm disturbances, mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to capture continuous electrocardiographic data over an extended period. This monitoring will assist in confirming the diagnosis and guiding appropriate rate or rhythm control management.

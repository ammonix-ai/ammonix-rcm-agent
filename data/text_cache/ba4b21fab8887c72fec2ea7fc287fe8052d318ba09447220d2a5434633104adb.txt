The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness, consistent with a history of paroxysmal atrial fibrillation and supraventricular tachycardia. These symptoms have persisted despite current medical management, prompting the need for extended ambulatory monitoring to correlate clinical events with rhythm disturbances. Mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to capture intermittent arrhythmias and assess their relationship to the patient's reported dizziness.

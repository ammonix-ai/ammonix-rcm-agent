The patient presents with recurrent episodes of transient neurological deficits, including brief visual disturbances and mild aphasia, consistent with a history of transient ischemic attacks and prior cerebral infarction. Given the concurrent diagnosis of nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency, there is concern for potential cardioembolic sources contributing to these ischemic events. Mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to evaluate for paroxysmal arrhythmias, such as atrial fibrillation, that may be precipitating these transient cerebral ischemic attacks.

The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and exertional dyspnea, raising concern for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation in the setting of known nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency. Given the intermittent nature of these symptoms and the need to correlate clinical events with rhythm disturbances, mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to capture arrhythmic episodes over an extended monitoring period. This study will assist in determining the burden of atrial fibrillation and guiding appropriate rate or rhythm control management.

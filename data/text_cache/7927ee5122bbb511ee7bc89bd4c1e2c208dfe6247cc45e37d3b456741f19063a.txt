The patient presents with a history of transient cerebral ischemic attacks and nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency, reporting recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness that are difficult to capture on standard electrocardiography. To evaluate for potential paroxysmal arrhythmias, such as atrial fibrillation, which may be contributing to these transient neurological symptoms and hemodynamic instability, a cardiac event monitoring study is indicated.

The patient presents with recurrent episodes of transient neurological deficits consistent with transient ischemic attacks, occurring against a background of personal history of cerebral infarction and nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency. Given the high suspicion for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation as a potential embolic source, long-term continuous ECG monitoring is ordered to detect intermittent arrhythmias that may not be captured on standard Holter monitoring.

The patient presents with recurrent episodes of presyncope and transient neurological deficits consistent with a recent transient cerebral ischemic attack, alongside documented bradyarrhythmias indicative of sick sinus syndrome. Given the clinical suspicion that paroxysmal atrial fibrillation or high-grade conduction disease is the underlying etiology for both the syncope and the ischemic events, a long-term continuous ECG monitoring study is ordered to capture the arrhythmic burden and correlate it with symptomatic episodes.

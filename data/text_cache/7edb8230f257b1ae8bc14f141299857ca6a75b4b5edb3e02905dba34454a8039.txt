The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and intermittent dizziness consistent with a history of sick sinus syndrome and supraventricular tachycardia. Standard short-term monitoring has failed to capture the arrhythmic events, necessitating extended ambulatory monitoring to correlate symptoms with rhythm disturbances. A long-term continuous ECG study is ordered to evaluate the frequency and hemodynamic significance of these arrhythmias for further management.

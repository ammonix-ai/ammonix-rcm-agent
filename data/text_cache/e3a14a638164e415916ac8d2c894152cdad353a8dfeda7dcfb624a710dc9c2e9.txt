The patient presents with a history of paroxysmal atrial fibrillation and prior transient ischemic attacks, currently experiencing intermittent palpitations and brief episodes of neurological symptoms suggestive of recurrent cerebral hypoperfusion. To evaluate for occult arrhythmic events that may be contributing to these transient ischemic symptoms and to guide long-term anticoagulation management, a 30-day continuous ambulatory ECG monitoring study is indicated.

The patient presents with a history of sick sinus syndrome and recurrent episodes of supraventricular tachycardia, complicated by intermittent atrial fibrillation. They report persistent palpitations, dizziness, and episodic syncope that have not been adequately characterized by standard ambulatory monitoring. Mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to capture continuous rhythm data and correlate these symptomatic events with underlying arrhythmias for further management.

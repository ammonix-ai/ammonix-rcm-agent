The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and transient neurological deficits consistent with paroxysmal atrial fibrillation and a recent transient cerebral ischemic attack. Given the history of unspecified cardiac arrhythmia and the need to correlate rhythm disturbances with symptomatic events, a long-term continuous ECG monitoring study is ordered to evaluate for atrial fibrillation burden and potential embolic sources.

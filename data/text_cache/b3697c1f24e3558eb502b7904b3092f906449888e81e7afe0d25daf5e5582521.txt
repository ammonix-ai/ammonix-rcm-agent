The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and intermittent lightheadedness, raising concern for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation and supraventricular tachycardia. Given the episodic nature of these symptoms and the need to correlate clinical events with rhythm disturbances, a 48-hour Holter monitor is ordered to capture potential arrhythmic episodes. This study will assist in confirming the diagnosis and guiding appropriate rate or rhythm control management.

The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness consistent with sick sinus syndrome, occurring in the context of known atherosclerotic heart disease. Recent transient cerebral ischemic attacks raise concern for paroxysmal atrial arrhythmias as a potential embolic source, necessitating prolonged rhythm monitoring to correlate symptoms with cardiac activity. Mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to capture intermittent dysrhythmias over an extended period to guide further management and anticoagulation decisions.

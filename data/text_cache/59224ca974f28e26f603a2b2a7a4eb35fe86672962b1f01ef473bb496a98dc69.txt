This correspondence serves to notify you that the claim for Mobile Cardiac Telemetry monitoring (CPT 93229) has been denied. The denial is based on Reason Code CO-197, indicating that the required precertification or prior authorization was not obtained before the service was rendered. Coverage for this diagnostic procedure mandates advance approval to ensure medical necessity and compliance with plan guidelines. As the authorization was absent, the claim cannot be processed for payment at this time. Please be advised that the member retains the right to appeal this determination. Detailed instructions regarding the appeals process, including necessary documentation and submission deadlines, are available in the member’s Summary of Benefits and Coverage or by contacting Member Services directly.

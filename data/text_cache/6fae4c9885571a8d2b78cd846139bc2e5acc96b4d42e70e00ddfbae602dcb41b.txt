The patient presents with a history of transient ischemic attacks and cerebral infarction, necessitating ongoing surveillance for potential atrial arrhythmias that may contribute to thromboembolic risk. Given the concurrent diagnosis of nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency, which can predispose to left atrial enlargement and subsequent rhythm disturbances, continuous monitoring is indicated to detect paroxysmal atrial fibrillation or other significant arrhythmias. This long-term study will help guide anticoagulation therapy and evaluate the hemodynamic impact of the valvular disease on cardiac rhythm.

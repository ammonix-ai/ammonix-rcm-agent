The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and intermittent lightheadedness, raising concern for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation in the context of a personal history of transient ischemic attack and cerebral infarction. A 48-hour Holter monitor is ordered to correlate these symptomatic events with cardiac rhythm disturbances and to assess the burden of atrial fibrillation for appropriate anticoagulation management.

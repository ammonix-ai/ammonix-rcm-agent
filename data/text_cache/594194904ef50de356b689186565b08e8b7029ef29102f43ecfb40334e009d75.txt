The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and transient neurological deficits suggestive of paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia and possible transient cerebral ischemic attacks. Given the history of symptomatic bradycardia consistent with sick sinus syndrome, a cardiac event monitor is ordered to correlate rhythm disturbances with clinical symptoms and evaluate for atrial arrhythmias contributing to the ischemic events.

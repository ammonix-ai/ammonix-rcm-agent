The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and dizziness consistent with supraventricular tachycardia and sick sinus syndrome, complicated by a recent history of transient cerebral ischemic attacks. Due to the intermittent nature of these arrhythmias and the need to correlate symptoms with cardiac rhythm disturbances, long-term continuous ECG monitoring is indicated to evaluate for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation or other significant conduction abnormalities.

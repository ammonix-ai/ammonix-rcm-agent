This correspondence serves to notify you that the claim for Mobile Cardiac Telemetry monitoring (CPT 93229) has been denied. The denial is based on Reason Code CO-197, indicating that the required precertification or prior authorization was not obtained before services were rendered. Lakeshore Advantage requires all such diagnostic monitoring services to be pre-approved to ensure medical necessity and coverage eligibility. As the provider, you have the right to appeal this determination. Please submit a formal appeal within the designated timeframe, including any supporting clinical documentation that justifies the medical necessity of the service. Failure to provide adequate justification may result in the finalization of this denial.

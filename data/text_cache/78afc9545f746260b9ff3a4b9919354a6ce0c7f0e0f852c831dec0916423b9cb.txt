The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness consistent with supraventricular tachycardia, occurring in the context of a personal history of transient ischemic attacks and cerebral infarction. Given the concern for paroxysmal atrial arrhythmias contributing to embolic risk, long-term continuous ECG monitoring is ordered to detect intermittent rhythm disturbances that may not be captured on standard ambulatory monitoring.

The patient presents with a history of sick sinus syndrome and recurrent episodes of supraventricular tachycardia, complicated by paroxysmal atrial fibrillation. They report persistent palpitations, intermittent dizziness, and unexplained syncope that have not been adequately characterized by standard ambulatory monitoring. Mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to capture real-time rhythm data and correlate symptoms with arrhythmic events for definitive management.

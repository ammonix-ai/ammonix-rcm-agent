The patient presents with intermittent palpitations and exertional dyspnea in the setting of known atherosclerotic heart disease and nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency. Given the history of unspecified cardiac arrhythmia and the need to correlate symptoms with potential rhythm disturbances over an extended period, mobile cardiac telemetry monitoring is indicated to evaluate for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation or other significant arrhythmias.

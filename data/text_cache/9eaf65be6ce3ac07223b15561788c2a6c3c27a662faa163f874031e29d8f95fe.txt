This correspondence serves to notify you that the claim for Mobile Cardiac Telemetry monitoring (CPT 93229) has been denied. The denial is based on Reason Code CO-197, indicating that the required precertification or prior authorization was not obtained before services were rendered. As this service requires advance approval to ensure medical necessity and coverage eligibility, the absence of valid authorization results in non-payment. Please be advised that you have the right to appeal this determination. To initiate an appeal, please submit a written request along with supporting clinical documentation within the timeframe specified in your plan’s Evidence of Coverage. Failure to adhere to these timelines may result in the forfeiture of appeal rights.

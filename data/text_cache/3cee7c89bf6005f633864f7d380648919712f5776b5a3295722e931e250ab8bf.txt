The patient presents with recurrent episodes of dizziness and transient visual disturbances concerning for transient cerebral ischemic attacks, occurring in the context of known sick sinus syndrome and unspecified cardiac arrhythmia. Due to the intermittent nature of these symptoms and the need to correlate clinical events with underlying rhythm disturbances, a cardiac event monitoring study is ordered to evaluate for potential arrhythmic causes of the cerebral hypoperfusion.

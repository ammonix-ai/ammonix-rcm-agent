The patient presents with a recent history of transient neurological deficits consistent with a transient cerebral ischemic attack, alongside persistent palpitations and lightheadedness suggestive of an underlying cardiac arrhythmia. To evaluate the temporal relationship between these events and identify potential paroxysmal atrial fibrillation or other rhythm disturbances, a long-term continuous ECG monitoring study is ordered. This diagnostic approach is necessary to correlate the patient's symptomatic episodes with specific cardiac electrical activity for appropriate risk stratification and management.

The patient presents with recurrent episodes of syncope and presyncope in the setting of known sick sinus syndrome, raising concern for intermittent high-grade atrioventricular block or severe bradycardia. Given the personal history of transient ischemic attack and cerebral infarction, continuous mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to correlate these symptomatic events with underlying arrhythmias and assess the efficacy of current rate-control management.

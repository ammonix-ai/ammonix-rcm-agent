The patient presents with a history of transient cerebral ischemic attacks and known atherosclerotic heart disease of the native coronary arteries, complicated by nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency. Due to the high risk of paroxysmal atrial fibrillation contributing to embolic events in the setting of valvular disease and prior TIA, long-term continuous ECG monitoring is indicated to detect intermittent arrhythmias that may not be captured on standard Holter monitoring.

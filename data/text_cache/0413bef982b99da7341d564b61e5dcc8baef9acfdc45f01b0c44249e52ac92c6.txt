The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and transient neurological deficits, including brief episodes of word-finding difficulty and visual blurring, consistent with a history of transient cerebral ischemic attacks. Given the clinical suspicion that these embolic events may be secondary to paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia, a 48-hour ambulatory ECG monitoring study is ordered to correlate cardiac rhythm disturbances with the onset of symptoms.

The patient presents with a history of paroxysmal atrial fibrillation and recent transient cerebral ischemic attacks, reporting intermittent palpitations and lightheadedness that suggest ongoing cardiac arrhythmia. A 48-hour Holter monitor is ordered to correlate these symptomatic episodes with rhythm disturbances and assess the efficacy of current rate control therapy. This evaluation aims to further characterize the unspecified cardiac arrhythmia and guide secondary stroke prevention strategies.

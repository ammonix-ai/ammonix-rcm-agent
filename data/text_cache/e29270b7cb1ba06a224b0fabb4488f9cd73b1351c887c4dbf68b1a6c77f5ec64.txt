The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and exertional dyspnea, consistent with a history of paroxysmal atrial fibrillation and underlying nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency. Given the concurrent diagnosis of sick sinus syndrome, which has manifested as intermittent bradycardia and lightheadedness, continuous ambulatory monitoring is required to correlate these symptoms with specific arrhythmic events. This mobile cardiac telemetry study is indicated to evaluate the burden of atrial fibrillation and assess for significant pauses or tachy-brady syndrome to guide further management.

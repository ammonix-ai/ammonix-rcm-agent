The patient presents with intermittent palpitations and a history of unexplained cardiac arrhythmia, prompting further evaluation to correlate symptoms with underlying rhythm disturbances. Mobile cardiac telemetry monitoring is ordered to capture continuous electrocardiographic data during daily activities, allowing for the detection of transient arrhythmic events that may not be evident on standard resting ECGs. This extended monitoring aims to clarify the etiology of the palpitations and guide appropriate therapeutic management.

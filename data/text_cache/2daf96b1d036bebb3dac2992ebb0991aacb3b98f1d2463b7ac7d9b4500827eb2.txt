The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness, raising concern for supraventricular tachycardia in the context of a personal history of transient ischemic attacks and cerebral infarction. Mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to correlate these symptomatic events with potential arrhythmic activity and to assess for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation or other tachyarrhythmias that may contribute to cerebral hypoperfusion.

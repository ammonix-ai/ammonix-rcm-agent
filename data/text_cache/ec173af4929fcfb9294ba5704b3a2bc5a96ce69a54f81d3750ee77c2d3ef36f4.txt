The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and transient neurological deficits, including brief episodes of word-finding difficulty and visual blurring, consistent with a history of paroxysmal atrial fibrillation and transient cerebral ischemic attacks. To evaluate the frequency and burden of arrhythmia and to correlate cardiac rhythm with these transient ischemic symptoms, a 30-day mobile cardiac telemetry monitoring study is ordered.

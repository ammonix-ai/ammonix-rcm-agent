The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and intermittent lightheadedness, raising concern for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation in the setting of known atherosclerotic heart disease. Given the sporadic nature of these symptoms and the need to correlate clinical events with rhythm disturbances, a cardiac event monitor is ordered to capture arrhythmic activity over an extended period. This study will help clarify the burden of atrial fibrillation and guide further management of his unspecified cardiac arrhythmias.

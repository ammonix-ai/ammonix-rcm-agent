The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and presyncope, with a history of documented supraventricular tachycardia and unspecified atral fibrillation. Given the recent syncopal events and the need to correlate symptoms with underlying arrhythmia, a long-term continuous ECG monitoring study is ordered to evaluate for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation or other tachyarrhythmias.

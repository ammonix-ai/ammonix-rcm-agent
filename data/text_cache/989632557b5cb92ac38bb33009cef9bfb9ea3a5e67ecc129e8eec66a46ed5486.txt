Following a comprehensive review of the submitted appeal materials, Prairie State Medicaid maintains its determination to deny coverage for the cardiac event monitoring study (CPT 93271). The documentation provided fails to demonstrate the requisite medical necessity as defined by current state-specific coverage criteria. Specifically, the clinical records do not substantiate that less intensive diagnostic alternatives were exhausted or that the patient’s symptoms warrant continuous ambulatory monitoring. Consequently, the claim remains ineligible for reimbursement under the beneficiary’s plan benefits. This decision is final regarding the current administrative level. Should new clinical evidence emerge that directly addresses the cited deficiencies, a new request for coverage may be submitted for independent evaluation.

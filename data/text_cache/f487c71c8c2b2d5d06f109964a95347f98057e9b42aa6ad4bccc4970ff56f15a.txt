The patient presents with recurrent episodes of syncope and presyncope in the setting of known nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency and unspecified atrial fibrillation. Due to the intermittent nature of these symptoms and the need to correlate hemodynamic events with arrhythmic activity, long-term continuous ECG monitoring is indicated to evaluate for potential bradyarrhythmias or high-burden atrial fibrillation contributing to the collapse.

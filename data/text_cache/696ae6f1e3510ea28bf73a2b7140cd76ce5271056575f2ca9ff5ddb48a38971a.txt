The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and intermittent lightheadedness, raising concern for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation in the setting of known atherosclerotic heart disease. Given the episodic nature of these symptoms and the need to correlate clinical events with rhythm disturbances, a cardiac event monitoring study is ordered to capture potential arrhythmic episodes. This evaluation will assist in confirming the diagnosis of atrial fibrillation and guiding appropriate anticoagulation and rate control management.

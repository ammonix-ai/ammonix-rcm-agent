The patient presents with recurrent episodes of dizziness and giddiness, raising concern for potential arrhythmia given their personal history of transient ischemic attack and cerebral infarction. Concurrent nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency further complicates the clinical picture, necessitating extended rhythm monitoring to evaluate for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation or other conduction abnormalities. Mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to capture intermittent symptomatic events and correlate them with cardiac rhythm disturbances over a prolonged period.

The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and intermittent lightheadedness, raising concern for paroxysmal atrial fibrillation in the setting of known atherosclerotic heart disease. Given the episodic nature of the symptoms and the need to correlate clinical events with rhythm disturbances, a cardiac event monitor is ordered to capture arrhythmic activity during symptomatic periods. This study will assist in confirming the diagnosis of atrial fibrillation and guiding appropriate rate or rhythm control management.

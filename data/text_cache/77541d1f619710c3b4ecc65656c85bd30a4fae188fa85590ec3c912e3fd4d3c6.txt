The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness, raising concern for paroxysmal arrhythmias in the setting of known nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency. Given the personal history of transient ischemic attack and cerebral infarction, prolonged rhythm monitoring is indicated to evaluate for atrial fibrillation or other thromboembolic sources. Mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to capture intermittent symptoms and correlate them with cardiac rhythm disturbances over an extended period.

The patient presents with a history of paroxysmal palpitations and episodic lightheadedness, raising concern for recurrent supraventricular tachycardia and undiagnosed atrial fibrillation. Given the recent transient cerebral ischemic attack, prolonged continuous ECG monitoring is indicated to detect intermittent arrhythmias that may have contributed to the cerebrovascular event and to guide appropriate anticoagulation and rate control therapy.

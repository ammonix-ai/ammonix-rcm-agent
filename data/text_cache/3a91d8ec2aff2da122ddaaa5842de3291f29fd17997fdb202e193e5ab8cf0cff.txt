The patient presents with a history of transient ischemic attacks and cerebral infarction, necessitating ongoing surveillance for potential paroxysmal atrial fibrillation as a contributing etiology. Given the concurrent diagnosis of nonrheumatic mitral valve insufficiency, which increases the risk of thromboembolic events, long-term continuous ECG monitoring is indicated to detect occult arrhythmias that may not be captured on standard 12-lead ECGs.

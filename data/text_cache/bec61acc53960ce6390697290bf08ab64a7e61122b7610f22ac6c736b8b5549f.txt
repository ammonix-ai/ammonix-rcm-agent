This notice confirms the denial of your claim for cardiac event monitoring (CPT 93271). The service is denied under reason code CO-22, indicating that care may be covered by another payer per coordination of benefits regulations. As the secondary payer, Prairie State Medicaid is not responsible for payment when another plan is primary. Please submit the Explanation of Benefits from the primary insurer to facilitate further review. You have the right to appeal this determination. To initiate an appeal, please submit a written request detailing the basis for your disagreement within the timeframe specified in your member handbook. Failure to appeal within the designated period may result in the loss of your right to contest this decision.

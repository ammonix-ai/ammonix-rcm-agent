The patient presents with recurrent episodes of palpitations and lightheadedness, raising concern for paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia and underlying atrial fibrillation. Given the intermittent nature of these symptoms and the need to correlate clinical events with rhythm disturbances, mobile cardiac telemetry is ordered to capture continuous electrocardiographic data over an extended period. This monitoring will assist in characterizing the arrhythmia burden and guiding appropriate therapeutic management for the unspecified cardiac arrhythmias.

Following a comprehensive review of the submitted clinical documentation and applicable coverage guidelines, we affirm the initial determination regarding the claim for cardiac event monitoring (CPT 93271). The provided records do not sufficiently demonstrate medical necessity as defined by current federal Medicare policy requirements. Specifically, the evidence fails to establish that less intensive monitoring methods were inadequate or that the patient’s symptoms warrant continuous ambulatory monitoring. Consequently, the denial stands. No further administrative appeals will be accepted for this specific claim. We recommend reviewing the official coverage determinations to ensure future submissions meet all requisite criteria for reimbursement.

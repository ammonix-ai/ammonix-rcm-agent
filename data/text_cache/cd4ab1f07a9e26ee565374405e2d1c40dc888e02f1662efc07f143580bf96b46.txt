Following a comprehensive review of the submitted clinical documentation and applicable coverage guidelines, we affirm the initial determination regarding the denial of the mobile cardiac telemetry monitoring service (CPT 93229). The provided records fail to demonstrate the requisite medical necessity, specifically lacking evidence of symptomatic arrhythmia or inadequate diagnostic yield from prior standard monitoring modalities. Consequently, the request does not meet the established criteria for coverage under the simulated Medicare program. This decision is final and constitutes the end of the administrative appeal process for this claim. No further review will be conducted unless new, material clinical information is presented that substantiates a change in the patient’s condition or diagnostic needs.
